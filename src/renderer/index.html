Add from link

Downloads a direct audio link, or a YouTube/video page (via yt-dlp), and adds it to your library. YouTube currently needs a browser picked under Settings → "YouTube/video downloads" first.

A safety cap — you can loop and crossfade a short clip in the app, so there's rarely a reason to pull a multi-hour stream in full. Set to 0 to download the whole thing.
